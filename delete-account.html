Account Deletion Request

How to Request Account Deletion

If you wish to delete your SmartRoads City Sense account and associated data, please follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to hello@smartroads.ai
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include your registered email address and username (if applicable) in the email body
  4. Our support team will process your request within 7 business days
  5. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been deleted

Quick Action: Click here to send a pre-filled deletion request email

Data Retention and Deletion

Data That Will Be Deleted:

  • Your account profile information (name, email, username)
  • User preferences and settings
  • Any uploaded content or user-generated data
  • Activity logs and usage history
  • Authentication credentials

Data That May Be Retained:

  • Transaction records: Retained for 7 years for accounting and tax compliance purposes
  • Legal and compliance data: Information required by law to be retained (e.g., fraud prevention, dispute resolution)
  • Aggregated/anonymized data: De-identified data used for analytics that cannot be traced back to you

Retention Period:

Most personal data will be deleted within 30 days of your deletion request being processed. Data subject to legal retention requirements will be securely stored and deleted once the retention period expires.

Important Notes

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
  • You will lose access to all services and data associated with your account
  • If you have active subscriptions, please cancel them before requesting deletion
  • Some data may be retained in backups for up to 90 days before being permanently purged

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about the account deletion process or data retention, please contact our support team at hello@smartroads.ai
